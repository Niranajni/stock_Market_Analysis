# stock_Market_Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analysing the Stock Market dataset using Python. It helps understand stock price movements, trading volumes, company performance, and industry-wise trends through data analysis and visualization.

## Objectives
- Analyse opening, closing, highest, and lowest stock prices.
- Identify companies with higher and lower stock prices.
- Study trading volume across different companies.
- Compare stock performance across industry categories.
- Understand stock market trends over time.
- Identify relationships between stock prices and trading volumes.

## Dataset Description
The dataset contains 10,818 records of 58 companies across 9 industry categories, covering January 2024 to September 2024.

**Dataset Columns:**
- Date – Trading date.
- Open – Opening stock price.
- High – Highest stock price.
- Low – Lowest stock price.
- Close – Closing stock price.
- Adj Close – Adjusted closing price.
- Volume – Number of shares traded.
- Symbol – Company stock symbol.
- Category – Industry category.

## Tools & Technologies Used
- Python
- Jupyter Notebook
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Analysis Performed
1. **Data Cleaning:** Checked missing values, data types, and duplicate records.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Analysed numerical and categorical data.
3. **Stock Price Analysis:** Compared opening, closing, highest, and lowest prices.
4. **Company Analysis:** Studied stock performance across different companies.
5. **Trading Volume Analysis:** Examined trading activity and volume patterns.
6. **Category Analysis:** Compared performance across 9 industry categories.
7. **Data Visualization:** Created charts and graphs using Matplotlib and Seaborn.

## Conclusion
This project provides insights into stock market performance through Python-based data analysis. It helps understand stock price movements, company performance, trading volumes, and industry-wise trends using statistical analysis and visualizations.
